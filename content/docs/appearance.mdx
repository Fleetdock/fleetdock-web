---
title: Appearance
description: Light, dark or system theme, the Classic or Neutral style, borderless panels and animations. Saved per browser.
---

**Settings → Appearance** changes how the dashboard looks for you. Choices are saved in this
browser and applied before the page shows, so nothing flashes on load. They do not affect other
people or other browsers.

| Setting | Choices |
| ------- | ------- |
| **Theme** | **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**, which follows your operating system and switches with it. The sun/moon button in the top bar toggles light and dark. |
| **Style** | **Classic**, the default, or **Neutral**: a compact gray style with light and dark variants. |
| **Borders** | **Borderless** removes the lines around panels and cards. Inputs and the data browser's tab strip keep theirs. |
| **Animations** | **Full**: pages fade in with a slight rise. **Reduced**: a plain fade. **Off**: no page or interface animations. Your system's reduce-motion setting always turns them off. |
